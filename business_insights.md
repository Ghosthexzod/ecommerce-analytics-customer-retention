# Business Insights

## 1\. West Region Generated the Highest Revenue

The West region had the strongest sales performance.

### Business Meaning

This suggests the West region has stronger customer demand and better purchasing activity.

### Recommended Action

The business should keep strong stock availability in this region and consider increasing marketing activity there.



## 2\. Electronics Was the Top Product Category

Electronics generated the highest revenue compared with other categories.

### Business Meaning

Electronics is a key revenue driver. It likely has higher unit prices and stronger customer demand.

### Recommended Action

The business should prioritise Electronics promotions, stock planning, and upselling opportunities.



## 3\. Delivery Performance Affected Customer Ratings

The analysis showed that delivery performance was connected to customer satisfaction.

### Business Meaning

Customers care about reliable and timely delivery. Poor delivery experience can reduce satisfaction and may increase churn risk.

### Recommended Action

The business should monitor delivery times and work on reducing delays.



## 4\. Repeat Orders Were Important for Customer Value

Customers with more orders were more likely to be high-value customers.

### Business Meaning

Repeat purchasing is one of the strongest signs of customer loyalty.

### Recommended Action

The business should create loyalty offers, repeat-purchase campaigns, and personalised promotions.



## 5\. Churn Risk Was Linked to Customer Experience

Customers with weaker ratings and lower engagement were more likely to fall into the high churn-risk group.

### Business Meaning

Customer experience has a direct relationship with customer retention.

### Recommended Action

The business should contact high-risk customers early and improve support, delivery, and post-purchase experience.



## 6\. Discounts Alone Were Not Enough

Discounts were not the strongest driver of retention.

### Business Meaning

Customers may buy once because of discounts, but long-term loyalty depends more on experience, delivery, and satisfaction.

### Recommended Action

The business should not rely only on discounts. It should also improve service quality and customer engagement.

## 

## Overall Business Conclusion

The project showed that the business can improve performance by focusing on:

* high-performing regions
* strong product categories
* better delivery experience
* repeat customer behaviour
* churn risk monitoring
* targeted retention strategies
